Privacy Policy & Google Play Data Safety

Privacy-by-Design for Pupils, Parents & Schools

Applies to the web platform at historyrevisionhub.co.uk and the official The History Revision Hub mobile app on Android and iOS.

Summary at a Glance

  • Zero Student Accounts: No sign-up, login, name, email address, or password is required or requested.
  • Zero Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not collect, transmit, store, or profile personal pupil data on our servers.
  • 100% Local-Device Progress: Quiz mastery, pinned subjects, reading tint preferences, and offline lessons stay solely on your own device and can be erased in one tap at any time.
  • Zero Behavioural Advertising or Data Selling: No third-party ad networks, cross-site trackers, or data brokers ever run inside the app.

1. Zero Student Accounts & Zero Personal Data Collection

The History Revision Hub is built as an open-access classroom and home revision companion for Key Stage 2, Key Stage 3, and GCSE History. Pupils can study lessons, listen to studio audio read-alouds, explore primary sources, and complete retrieval quizzes immediately without creating an account.

We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, home addresses, telephone numbers, school roll identifiers, photographs, microphone recordings, contacts, or precise device geolocation.

2. 100% Local-Device Storage & Offline Caching

To help pupils pick up where they left off without an account, the app uses standard browser storage (localStorage, sessionStorage, and the Service Worker Cache API) strictly on your own phone, tablet, or computer:

  • Spaced-Retrieval Mastery: Leitner box progress and question review timestamps so due weak-spot questions resurface automatically.
  • Pinned Courses & Bookmarks: Your chosen History units and most recently opened lesson.
  • Accessibility & Audio Settings: Visual-stress reading overlays, font preferences, and continuous audio playback toggles.
  • Offline Lesson Packs: Curriculum JSON and static assets you choose to save for offline study.

None of this study state is uploaded to a central user database. Clearing your browser site data, uninstalling the Android/iOS app, or pressing the button below permanently deletes all local app storage on this device.

Erase Local Study Data on This Device
Clears local quiz mastery, pinned units, preferences, and offline caches immediately.

3. Zero Third-Party Behavioural Advertising or Data Sharing

The History Revision Hub contains zero advertisements, zero third-party ad SDKs, zero social-media tracking pixels, and zero cross-app profiling. We never sell, rent, or trade user information to third parties.

Standard web requests to historyrevisionhub.co.uk are served over encrypted HTTPS solely to deliver static curriculum pages, historical images, PDFs, and studio narration audio.

4. Age-Appropriate Content, UK GDPR & Children’s Code Compliance

All curriculum materials are authored for school-age History learners (ages 9–16+) aligned with the English National Curriculum (KS2 & KS3) and Pearson Edexcel GCSE (9–1) History specifications. Historical topics involving conflict, medicine, or persecution are presented in an age-calibrated academic manner appropriate for UK classroom study.

By operating without accounts, behavioural profiling, nudge mechanics, or personal data collection, the platform upholds data minimisation by default under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), the Data Protection Act 2018, and the ICO Age Appropriate Design Code (UK Children’s Code).
