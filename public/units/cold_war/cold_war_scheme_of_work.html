Subject Knowledge Primer (Non-Specialist Walk-In Briefing)

This enquiry investigates the thirteen days in October 1962 when humanity came closest to thermonuclear war: the Cuban Missile Crisis. Ground the crisis firmly in its causal build-up—Fidel Castro’s January 1959 overthrow of Batista, US economic sanctions, the CIA-backed Bay of Pigs fiasco (April 1961), and American Jupiter missiles already stationed in Turkey and Italy—so Year 9 pupils understand why Khrushchev launched secret 'Operation Anadyr' rather than viewing Soviet missiles in Cuba as an irrational bolt from the blue.

Core Learning Objectives
  • Explain why Cuba became a Soviet ally after 1959 and why Khrushchev secretly deployed nuclear missiles there in 1962.
  • Analyse how President Kennedy managed the Thirteen Days (14–28 October 1962) without triggering nuclear war.
  • Assess the stabilizing consequences of the crisis for superpower nuclear deterrence after 1962.

Subject Knowledge Primer (Non-Specialist Walk-In Briefing)

This synoptic capstone enquiry challenges George Orwell’s famous metaphor—asking Year 9 historians whether the Cold War was ever truly 'cold.'
