The Kingdom of Benin

“How did the rainforest Kingdom of Benin build giant earthworks, a sacred royal court, and world-famous brass sculptures?”
3 Clues to Spot on This Benin Plaque: • Shining golden brass cast in clay moulds using the clever lost-wax method on Igun Street.
• Sacred coral-bead collars, patterned shields, and ceremonial swords showing royal court rank.
• Corner nail holes proving this plaque once hung on a tall wooden pillar inside the Oba’s palace!
Think About This: Why did Edo communities in southern Nigeria dig 16,000 kilometres of red-clay earthworks and light wide streets with palm-oil lamps centuries ago?
