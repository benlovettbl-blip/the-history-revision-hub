Crime & Punishment Through Time

“How and why have crimes, policing, trials, and punishments changed across 2,000 years of British history?”
3 Clues to Spot on Lady Justice: • Balanced golden scales weighing courtroom evidence fairly for both sides.
• An unsheathed sword showing the lawful authority of courts and juries.
• Standing high above London’s Old Bailey — with open eyes watching the city!
Think About This: Why did Britain have zero uniformed police officers for nearly 1,800 years from Roman Bath until Sir Robert Peel’s 1829 London "Bobbies"?
