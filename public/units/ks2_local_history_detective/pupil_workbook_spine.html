History on Our Doorstep

“How can maps, buildings, archives, memorials, and living memories unlock the history on our own doorstep?”
3 Doorstep Clues to Spot Right Away: • A stone river crossing and ancient church tower marking centuries of village settlement.
• Steep gabled weavers’ cottages built from local stone long before modern brick suburbs.
• Zero shop signs needed — every roof, bridge, and street name hides real historical clues!
