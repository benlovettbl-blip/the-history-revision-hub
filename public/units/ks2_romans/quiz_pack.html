Enquiry 1: Celtic Britain Before the Romans (c. 500 BC–AD 43)
Q1. How was Iron Age Britain ruled before the Romans arrived? [ By dozens of separate tribes each ruling their own lands / By one single King of all Britain in London ]
Q2. Which massive Iron Age hillfort in Dorset was ringed with steep earth ramparts and wooden fences?
Q3. In which two years did the Roman general Julius Caesar first land in Britain before sailing back to Gaul?
Q4. Which Iron Age tribe lived in eastern Britain, while the Brigantes lived in the north?
Enquiry 2: Emperor Claudius Invades Britain (AD 43)
Q5. Why did powerful Roman senators doubt Emperor Claudius when he became ruler in AD 41? [ He walked with a limp, spoke with a stutter, and had never led an army / He lived in Britain instead of Italy ]
Q6. Which underground metals did Rome want to mine in Britain for coins, water pipes, and weapons?
Q7. Besides metals and wheat, what payments would conquered British tribes have to hand over to Rome?
Q8. In which year did Emperor Claudius launch the full-scale Roman invasion of Britain?
Enquiry 3: Boudicca's Iceni Rebellion (AD 60–61)
Q9. Which tribe in East Anglia did King Prasutagus and Queen Boudicca rule? [ The Iceni / The Brigantes ]
Q10. To which Roman Emperor did King Prasutagus leave half of his wealth in AD 60, hoping Rome would protect his daughters?
Q11. What did greedy Roman officials do instead of keeping King Prasutagus's peaceful agreement?
Q12. Which neighbouring British tribe joined the Iceni to fight alongside Queen Boudicca?

Enquiry 4: Hadrian's Wall & Frontier Soldiers (AD 122–400)
Q13. In what year did Emperor Hadrian visit Britain and order his northern stone wall to be built? [ AD 122 / 55 BC ]
Q14. How long was Hadrian's Wall from Wallsend on the River Tyne to Bowness-on-Solway?
Q15. What was built at every Roman mile along Hadrian's Wall to stop raids and collect taxes from traders?
Q16. How were the communal multi-seat stone latrines at Housesteads Roman Fort kept clean?
Enquiry 5: Life in Roman Towns — Roads, Baths & Villas
Q17. What wooden sighting tool did Roman surveyors use to line up dead-straight highways? [ A groma / A bronze strigil ]
Q18. Roughly how many miles of stone highways did the Romans build across Britain?
Q19. Why were Roman road surfaces cambered (gently curved) with deep ditches along both sides?
Q20. What stood at the centre of a planned Roman town as an open marketplace, alongside a basilica town hall?
Enquiry 6: Farewell to the Legions & Roman Legacy (AD 410 & Beyond)
Q21. Which groups raided Roman Britain from Scotland, Ireland, and across the North Sea around AD 400? [ Picts, Scotti, and Angles and Saxons / Vikings, Normans, and Mongols ]
Q22. Why were Roman legions called away from Britain in the early fifth century?
Q23. In what year did Emperor Honorius send his famous reply telling British towns to defend themselves?
Q24. What message did Emperor Honorius send to the cities of Britain in AD 410?
