THE ROMANS IN BRITAIN: TEACHER GUIDE & SOW

Complete Walk-In Planning Pack • 6 Modular 3-Act Enquiries (55 BC–AD 410) • 100% Commercially Cleared Sources

1. Statutory National Curriculum Alignment & 3-Act Primary Structure

Fulfils the DfE Key Stage 2 statutory study of “the Roman Empire and its impact on Britain”. Rather than secondary-school 5-stage essays, every enquiry uses 3 vivid narrative acts (~310 words total) designed for finger-tracked classroom reading alongside archaeological detective work.

2. Universal Single-Tier Historian Scaffolding Across Key Stage 2

  • Phonetic Vocabulary & Clear Primary Prose: Every enquiry opens with phonetic Syllable Key Words so every pupil can pronounce and use specialist historical terms confidently.
  • One Universal Scaffold for All Pupils: Every extended writing task provides 1 universal Sentence Starter and 1 Historian Word Bank, paired with an aspirational Model Historian Answer (WAGOLL) for whole-class feedback.

3. Complete Print & Digital Classroom Suite

  • 12-Page Classroom Textbook: Reusable single-column reading book (3 folded A3 sheets).
  • 16-Page Master Pupil Workbook OR 6 Standalone 2-Page A4 Worksheets: Flexible write-in options.
  • 4-Page Retrieval Companion (24 Questions) + Interactive Chronology Slider & Oracy Arena.

Learning Objectives:
  • Explain how Iron Age Britain was divided into separate Celtic tribes protected by earth hillforts.
  • Describe everyday family life and clever engineering inside a thatched roundhouse.
  • Use archaeological artefacts and Julius Caesar's account to prove that the Celts were skilled craftworkers and sea traders.
Model Big Question Answer (WAGOLL): “Before the Roman legions arrived in AD 43, the Iron Age Celts were far from backward because they were clever builders, skilled farmers, master smiths, and international sea traders. Learning Objectives:
