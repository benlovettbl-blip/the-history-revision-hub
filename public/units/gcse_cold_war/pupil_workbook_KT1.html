Audio • Quiz Hub
US Air Force Official Photographer (1948–1949) US National Archives (NARA)
Cover Source Study 1948–1949
Operation Vittles — C-54 Skymaster Approaching Tempelhof, West Berlin
During Stalin’s eleven-month blockade of West Berlin (June 1948 – May 1949), Allied aircraft flew over 275,000 flights along three narrow air corridors to sustain two million civilians without firing a shot.
3 Details to Spot in This Photograph:
1. Four-engine US Air Force C-54 ("TROOP CARRIER") with landing gear lowered over ruined Berlin apartments.
2. Crowds of West Berlin children standing atop post-war rubble mounds cheering incoming food and coal supplies.
3. Showed how Truman upheld containment peacefully without firing on Soviet forces.
Class Discussion: Why was Stalin unwilling to order Soviet fighter planes to shoot down unarmed Western cargo aircraft over East Germany?

Significance: arms race; formation of the Warsaw Pact
Events in 1956 leading to the Hungarian Uprising & Khrushchev’s response
International reaction to the Soviet invasion of Hungary
