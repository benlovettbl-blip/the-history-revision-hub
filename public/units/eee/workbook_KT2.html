Catholic Plots & Mary’s Execution, Anglo-Spanish Rivalry, Outbreak of War, and Defeat of the Armada
Audio • Quiz Hub
Attributed to George Gower (c. 1588) Woburn Abbey Collection, Bedfordshire
Cover Source Study c. 1588
The Armada Portrait of Queen Elizabeth I (c. 1588)
Painted straight after the defeat of Philip II’s invasion fleet in August 1588, Gower’s portrait celebrates Protestant deliverance and England’s emerging Atlantic ambition.
3 Details to Spot in This Source:
1. Right hand resting over the Americas on the globe, signalling English imperial ambition.
2. Left window depicting English fireships advancing across calm sunlit waters.
3. Right window showing Spanish Armada galleons wrecked on storm-lashed rocky cliffs.
Class Discussion: How did the Armada Portrait turn England’s narrow naval survival in August 1588 into a powerful myth of Protestant triumph and global reach?
Q1 • Two Features 4m (2×2m)
Two separate 2-mark features (1a & 1b). Identify a valid feature (1m) + add 1 sentence of specific factual detail (1m) (5 mins).
Q2 • Explain Why 12 Marks
3 PEEL causation paragraphs explaining why. Include own knowledge beyond the 2 stimulus points — zero storytelling (18 mins).
Q3 / Q4 • How Far Agree 16 Marks
Choose Q3 OR Q4 (zero SPaG). Write 3 balanced paragraphs (for & against + own knowledge) and a clear judgement (27 mins).

Spanish invasion plans. Key events of the Spanish Armada.
The reasons for the English victory.
