1. British Withdrawal & Creation of Israel
  • Conflicting interests and demands of Jews and Arabs within the British Mandate.
  • End of the British Mandate, partition and creation of Israel: King David Hotel bombing (1946) and UN Resolution 181 (1947).
  • Key events of the Arab-Israeli war (1948–49).
2. Aftermath of the 1948–49 War
  • Territorial changes and their impact (1949 Armistice Green Line).
  • The refugee status of Palestinian Arabs.
  • Creation of the Israeli Defence Forces (IDF) and the Law of Return (1950).
  • US aid to Israel and Israel’s relations with Egypt.
3. Increased Tension, 1955–63
  • Nasser and Egypt’s leadership of the Arab world.
  • Events and significance of Israeli attacks on Gaza (1955) and Sinai (1956).
  • Events and significance of the Suez Crisis (1956) and formation of the UAR (1958).
