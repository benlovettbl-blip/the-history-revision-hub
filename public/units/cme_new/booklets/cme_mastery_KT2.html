1. The Six Day War, 1967
  • Significance of the Cairo Conference (1964) and growth of Fatah and the PLO.
  • Escalating tension: Syria’s support for Fatah, Samu raid and events of 7 April 1967.
  • Actions of the USSR, Nasser and the USA leading to war.
  • Key events of the Six Day War (June 1967).
2. Aftermath of the 1967 War
  • UN Resolution 242 and the continued dispute over the Suez Canal.
  • Palestinian refugees and significance of the occupied territories (Golan, Gaza, West Bank, Sinai, East Jerusalem).
  • PFLP plane hijacks (1970), Black September, Munich Olympics (1972) and expulsion of PLO from Jordan.
3. Israel and Egypt, 1967–73
  • Egyptian relations with Israel, the USA, the USSR and Arab states.
  • Israel’s consolidation of control of the occupied territories.
  • Key events of the Yom Kippur War (1973) and its aftermath.
