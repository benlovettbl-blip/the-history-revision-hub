Guide 02: The Master Cover Engine & Commercial Customizer

Standardized Cover Architecture, Provenance Metadata & Multi-School Branding
Executive Summary: All printed pupil workbooks enforce the Master Cover Engine (scripts/components/render_standard_cover.cjs). This guarantees uncropped archival artwork presentation, curriculum alignment, and 100% institutional neutrality so any adopting school can stamp its own department name cleanly.
🏛️ Front Cover Architecture (Page 1)
  • Commercial Customizer Hook: Encloses <span class="school-brand-target">The History Department</span> inside data-department-name for multi-school branding.
  • Responsive Uncropped Artwork Gate: Automatically selects landscape showcase or split-portrait framing with object-fit: contain and verified museum accession metadata.
  • Pedagogical Anchor Deck: Displays 4 Core Features, 3-Pillar Visual Matrix, Disciplinary Significance statement, and a Targeted Hinge Question.
  • Pupil Identification Block: Clean Name, Class, and blank Teacher fields (zero school identifiers; zero GCSE target-grade clutter on KS3 covers).
  • Course Specification Directory: Maps unit enquiries directly to National Curriculum or Pearson Edexcel specification points at 0px overflow.
📊 Back Cover Architecture (Final Page)
  • Assessment Pathway Benchmarks: Displays transparent KS3 percentage bands (Em 35%, Em+ 50%, Ex 65%, Ex+ 80%, GD 90%) or GCSE Edexcel grade descriptors.
  • 6-Row Marking & Progress Grid: Tracks core unit enquiries plus one designated teacher assessment task cleanly on one scannable ledger.
  • Teacher Sign-Off & Verification: Dedicated signature/date spaces supporting low-workload classroom perimeter checks.
  • Permanent Vector QR Hub: Crisp vector SVG QR codes linking directly to historyrevisionhub.co.uk interactive lessons, audio read-alouds, and self-marking quizzes.
  • Automated Page-Budget Gate: Audited in headless Chromium to guarantee ≥ 95% space utilization and zero trailing spillover pages.
Commercial Multi-School Customizer Pattern

To uphold strict commercial independence, hardcoded school names are prohibited across the codebase. Adopting schools can customize booklet covers via three supported hooks:

1. Browser Query Param: Append ?school=St+Peters+Collegiate when opening any HTML booklet.
2. CLI Build Environment Variable: SCHOOL_NAME="St Jude's Academy" node scripts/render_<unit>_workbook.cjs
3. Stylesheet Override: Target .school-brand-target or [data-department-name] prior to print export.
