Guide 01: The Curricular Separation-of-Concerns Model

Architecture & 1-to-1 Alignment Across Textbooks, Pupil Workbooks, Retrieval Companions & The Web App
Executive Summary: The History Revision Hub separates reading, writing, spaced retrieval, and multimedia enquiry into distinct, synchronized resources. Each lesson shares an identical 5-Stage Chronological Spine (Stage 1–Stage 5) across screen and print—eliminating split-attention, preventing numbering drift, and cutting reprographics waste.
📖 Classroom Textbook (Reusable)
Role: Durable classroom reading anthology.

Key Features:
  • 100% pure disciplinary narrative prose read verbatim from live unit JSON data.
  • Balanced 2-column Lora serif measure at KS3/GCSE (single wide-margin column at KS2).
  • Clean [1.1]–[1.5] block headers with zero in-prose bracket pills.
  • Zero source boxes, zero margin glossary strips, and zero task lines.
  • Outside back-cover oral/notebook Knowledge Retrieval Quiz (zero answers printed).
📝 Pupil Workbook (Consumable)
Role: Termly saddle-stitched A4 writing pack.

Key Features:
  • Pure #000000 monochrome interior for crisp, zero-dither school photocopying.
  • Verso (Left): 5 full-width stacked Stage Question boxes (≤ 82 chars, 5 ruled lines each).
  • Recto (Right): Genre-calibrated extended/exam writing (~8mm pitch) with content-free Analytical Connectives: and unassigned Key Terms:.
  • Zero in-workbook Do Nows and zero factual giveaways in stems.
  • Center-Spine Specification Mind-Map Canvas + British Exercise Notes pages.
💻 Interactive Web App (Digital Hub)
Role: Multimedia classroom & home revision engine.

Key Features:
  • 3-Mode Spaced-Retrieval Do Now engine (strictly prior lessons/units).
  • Studio Historian UK audio (0.85x / 1.0x / 1.15x) with sentence/word karaoke tracking & visual-stress overlays.
  • Alternating visual plates + 5 rotated primary sources (A–E) with Hinge Questions.
  • In-place Vocabulary & Key Individual Dossier popovers + 1-Page Visual Story Sheets.
  • Tap-Tile Summary Clozes, Self-Marking Quizzes, Universal Arcade & Exam Practice Zone.
Why Separation of Concerns Prevents Curriculum Drift

Alongside companion Master Knowledge Retrieval Companions ("Quiz Packs") and Mastery Exam Packs, keeping pure reading in the reusable Textbook while housing active writing in the Workbook and interactive scaffolding in the Web App solves two chronic departmental problems:

1-to-1 Stage Synchronization Invariant: Every lesson's 5 narrative blocks, 5 Web App Chronology Spine cards, and 5 Printed Workbook Verso boxes are locked together by automated SHA-1 prose-hash verification (npm run audit:stages). Editing any narrative paragraph automatically flags its paired Stage Question for verification before PDF compilation.
