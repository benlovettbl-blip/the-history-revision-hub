Chronological Causal Domino Spine (1919–1922)
12 Sep 1919

Hitler Infiltrates and Joins Anton Drexler's DAP

Army informant Hitler sent to spy on the 55-member Munich workers' group; impressed by Drexler, Hitler joins and takes charge of party propaganda.

24 Feb 1920

25-Point Programme & Party Renamed to NSDAP

Hofbräuhaus rally proclaims the 25 points; party adopts the hooked swastika flag (Hakenkreuz) and Roman salute to create distinctive visual branding.

17 Dec 1920

Purchase of the Völkischer Beobachter Newspaper

Secret army funds provided by Ernst Röhm allow the NSDAP to buy its first newspaper, giving Hitler a mass media organ across Bavaria.

29 Jul 1921

Hitler Ousts Drexler & Seizes Total Dictatorial Control

Hitler establishes the Führerprinzip (Leader Principle), demanding absolute obedience and eliminating all internal democratic committee votes.

Nov 1921

The Sturmabteilung (SA) Formally Established

Paramilitary squad under Ernst Röhm deployed as political muscle to guard Nazi meetings and violently attack SPD and Communist opponents.

Disciplinary Enquiry & Exam Mastery
Key Topic 2.1 Hinge Question
"Why was the combination of the 25-Point Programme and the SA uniquely dangerous compared to traditional right-wing fringe parties in Weimar Germany?"
Grade 9 Disciplinary Benchmark (8/8 Marks)
The early NSDAP proved uniquely dangerous because it synthesized two revolutionary ingredients that traditional German nationalist groups lacked: ideological cross-class appeal and institutionalised paramilitary violence. Traditional right-wing parties (like the DNVP) were aristocratic, monarchist, and elitist, alienating ordinary working-class Germans. In contrast, Hitler and Drexler’s 25-Point Programme combined visceral anti-Versailles nationalism with populist socialist appeals (profit-sharing, land reform, abolition of unearned incomes) designed specifically to detach workers from the SPD and KPD.

Simultaneously, Ernst Röhm’s Sturmabteilung (SA) transformed street violence from spontaneous brawling into a disciplined political weapon. By violently seizing control of public spaces, protecting Nazi meetings, and terrorising left-wing opponents, the SA projected an illusion of orderly paramilitary strength amidst Weimar’s post-war chaos. Supported by Hitler’s charismatic beer-hall oratory and media reach via the Völkischer Beobachter, this lethal fusion of radical populist rhetoric and organized street violence established the NSDAP as a dynamic, predatory force capable of rapid mobilization.
Paper 3 Disciplinary Tip: Oratory vs Organisation

In Edexcel 12-mark causation questions, avoid attributing early Nazi growth solely to Hitler’s speaking talent. Top candidates separate charismatic factors (beer-hall oratory, theatrical pauses, patriotic grievance) from structural factors (the 25-Point manifesto, the swastika symbol, the SA paramilitary wing, and the Völkischer Beobachter newspaper).
