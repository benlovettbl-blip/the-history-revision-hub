Disciplinary Enquiry & Exam Mastery
Key Topic 2.4 Hinge Question
"Why did conservative elites like Franz von Papen believe they could 'tame' Hitler in January 1933, and why was their calculation fatally mistaken?"
Grade 9 Disciplinary Benchmark (8/8 Marks)
Conservative elites, led by Franz von Papen and President Hindenburg’s inner circle (the Camarilla), genuinely believed they could exploit Hitler as a charismatic figurehead while holding all the substantive levers of state power. Their calculation rested on three structural safeguards:

1. Institutional Numerical Isolation: Only three of the eleven cabinet ministers were Nazis (Hitler, Frick, and Göring). The remaining eight portfolios—including the Foreign Office, Defence, and Finance—were held by aristocratic conservatives. As Vice-Chancellor and Minister-President of Prussia, Papen held the unique constitutional right to attend every private meeting between Hitler and Hindenburg, leading him to famously boast: "We have hired him!"

2. Presidential and Military Veto: Field Marshal von Hindenburg retained the supreme office of President, the sole authority to sign Article 48 emergency decrees, and supreme command over the regular German Army (Reichswehr). The elites assumed that if Hitler stepped out of line, Hindenburg could instantly dismiss him as he had done to Brüning, Papen, and Schleicher.

Why their calculation was fatally mistaken: Papen completely underestimated Hitler’s fanaticism, revolutionary ruthlessness, and armed street muscle (400,000 SA stormtroopers). Crucially, the elites ceded the portfolio of Prussian Minister of the Interior to Hermann Göring. This gave the Nazis direct executive control over the Prussian state police—Germany’s largest police force of 50,000 armed men. Within weeks, Göring deputized the SA as auxiliary police, neutralising the state’s ability to resist and setting the stage for total dictatorial consolidation following the Reichstag Fire.
Paper 3 Disciplinary Tip: Popularity vs Intrigue

In Edexcel 16-mark essays on Hitler’s appointment as Chancellor, never argue that Hitler won power purely through democratic election. Remember that in November 1932, Nazi votes fell from 230 to 196 seats! High-scoring answers prove that Hitler was rescued from political decline by the selfish backstairs intrigue and fatal underestimation of conservative elites.
