# Engaging Pupil Workbooks (KS3 & GCSE) — Topic-First Recto Design Plan

> **Core Principle:** Pedagogy and authentic historical thinking always come before rigid page templates or mechanical task rotation.

## 1. The 4-Rule Topic-First Recto Plan
1. **Form Follows the History (No Blind Auto-Rotation):**
   Never rotate mechanically through a fixed task menu. For every lesson, ask: *What is the most authentic, engaging, high-thinking task a historian should do with THIS specific topic?*
   - **Propaganda / Source / Coin lessons:** Split-page **Top = Source/Plate Annotation** (circle bias, underline clues, margin detective prompts) → **Bottom = Short Structured Write-Up** built straight off those annotations.
   - **Conquest / Empire / Battle lessons:** Clean monochrome outline map or tactical diagram with route arrows, shading, and 2–3 sharp causal callouts.
   - **Controversy / Usurpation lessons:** Evidence Weigh-Up (*Prosecution vs Defence* / *Myth vs Archaeology*) + a concise 4-line verdict.
   - **Causation / Turning-Point lessons:** Visual weighting (pie chart, scales, or tension/fortune line) + 2–3 sentences defending the decisive factor.
   - **Justifiable Historical Drawing & Schematic Tasks (Top Half):** Whenever sketching *is* the clearest way to understand the history (e.g. drawing and labelling a cross-section of a Western Front trench, Pasargadae's four-quarter *pairidaeza* garden & water channels, or the trireme chokepoint at Salamis), give the pupil a clean framed drawing canvas in the top half paired with 3–4 crisp historical labels/explanations underneath.
   - **Natural Capstone Essays (Let It Breathe):** Whenever a lesson—especially towards the end of a unit—genuinely calls for a big extended essay or GCSE judgement, let that happen naturally on the right-hand side (or a full 4-page double-spread) without forcing gimmicks.
2. **Exam-Board Stem Purity (Never Mix Boards):**
   - **Edexcel GCSE (`1HI0`):** *"How useful are Sources A and B for an enquiry into..."* (8m), *"Explain why..."* (12m), *"How far do you agree..."* (16m + 4 SPaG).
   - **OCR Ancient History (`J198`):** Period Study *"Using details from Passage A and your own knowledge, what can we learn / explain how..."* (`5m` Q3/Q4), *"Outline..."* (`6m`), *"Explain..."* (`10m`), *"To what extent..."* (`15m`/`20m`); Depth Study *"How accurate do you think [author]'s account of... is?"* (`5m`), `20m + 5 SPaG` source-backed essay.
3. **Whole-Unit Rhythm Check (Human Judgement):**
   Only *after* designing every lesson bespoke to its topic, review the full unit sequence to prevent accidental 3-in-a-row heavy writing fatigue while letting end-of-unit synthesis essays breathe naturally.
4. **Flexible Page Budget (Saddle-Stitch Friendly):**
   Saddle-stitching only requires the **total** booklet page count to be a multiple of 4 (`16, 20, 24, 28, 32`). Use 2-page spreads by default, and use 4-page double-spreads (or paired 3p + 1p spreads) whenever flagship lessons need space.

## 2. Zero-Waste Back-of-Booklet Spare-Page Toolkit (1–3 Spare Pages)
- **1 Spare Page:** **Green-Pen Feedback & Assessment Redraft Page** *(My Target → Upgraded Paragraph)* **or** **20-Question End-of-Unit Retrieval Quiz** *(or OCR: Ancient Authors & SPaG Reference Sheet)*.
- **2 Spare Pages:** **Left = Whole-Unit Retrieval Quiz** · **Right = Green-Pen Redraft Page** *(or OCR Authors & Prescribed Sources Reference)*.
- **3 Spare Pages:** **1. Unit Overview / Authors Reference** → **2. Whole-Unit Retrieval Quiz** → **3. Green-Pen Redraft Page**.

## 3. Guardrails (Zero AI Trash & Pupil-Owned Thinking)
- **Zero AI Trash:** Strictly zero corporate/edutainment jargon, zero cheesy filler boxes, and zero gimmickry. Every word on the page must be crisp, authentic history.
- **No Over-Scaffolding on Printed Creative Tasks:** Never do the thinking for the pupil on paper. Give them a clean, generous workspace (blank framed drawing box, clean unlabelled outline map, open margin around a source, or clean lines) with a short, sharp task instruction—NOT pre-filled arrows, heavy sentence-starter crutches, or spoon-fed fill-in-the-blank boxes. Keep optional guidance, hints, and model breakdowns **inside the web app** for teachers and pupils who need them, so the printed workbook stays genuinely pupil-owned.
- **Frozen Units Guardrail:** Keep frozen KS3 classroom desk-copy units untouched until their unfreeze date (`24 Dec 2026`).
- Ideal first pilot: **OCR GCSE Ancient History** workbooks (and a single non-frozen KS3 pilot when ready).
