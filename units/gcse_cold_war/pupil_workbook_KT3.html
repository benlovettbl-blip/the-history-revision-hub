Cover Source Study November 1989
Citizens Atop the Berlin Wall Before the Brandenburg Gate
Following Mikhail Gorbachev’s repudiation of the Brezhnev Doctrine and East Germany’s sudden border opening on 9 November 1989, crowds climbed freely onto the Berlin Wall in front of the Brandenburg Gate without a single Soviet tank intervening.
3 Details to Spot in This Photograph:
1. Hundreds of East and West Berliners standing shoulder-to-shoulder atop the rounded cap of the Berlin Wall.
2. The Brandenburg Gate and tall border floodlight tower rising directly behind the celebrating crowd.
3. Hand-painted "LOVE & PEACE IN WEST & EAST" banners taped across the graffiti-covered western wall face.
Class Discussion: Did the Cold War end primarily because of Ronald Reagan’s military pressure or Mikhail Gorbachev’s internal reforms?
