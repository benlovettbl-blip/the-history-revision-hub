Enquiry 1: Cities in the Rainforest — How Did the Maya Build Thriving Cities in Mesoamerica? (c. 2000 BC–AD 600)
Q1. Which five modern countries make up the ancient Maya homeland in Mesoamerica?
Q2. What razor-sharp black volcanic glass did Maya workers dig from the southern highlands?
Q3. What are the deep natural limestone sinkholes used as freshwater wells across northern Yucatán called?
Q4. Which three crops grew together in clever Mesoamerican "Three Sisters" companion planting?
Enquiry 2: Step Pyramids & Jade Kings — How Were Rival Maya City-States Ruled? (c. AD 250–900)
Q5. How was the Classic Maya world organised politically between AD 250 and 900?
Q6. What did the royal Maya title "K'uhul Ajaw" mean?
Q7. How old was K'inich Janaab' Pakal when he became ruler of Palenque in AD 615, and how long did he reign?
Q8. What dazzling treasure did archaeologists uncover inside King Pakal’s tomb beneath the Temple of the Inscriptions?
Enquiry 3: Glyphs, Bark-Paper Books & Zero — Why Were Maya Scribes & Astronomers So Brilliant? (c. AD 300–900)
Q9. Roughly how many word and syllable hieroglyphs could Classic Maya scribes combine to write their language? [ Over 800 glyphs / Only 10 signs ]
Q10. What historical name is given to Maya accordion-folded books, and what material were their pages made from?
Q11. In Maya base-20 mathematics, what values did a shell, a pebble dot, and a wooden bar stand for?
Q12. What was "El Caracol" at the Maya city of Chichén Itzá?

Enquiry 4: Maize Tortillas, Frothy Cacao & Pok-ta-Pok — What Was Everyday Maya Life Like? (c. AD 600–900)
Q13. What volcanic stone grinding tool did Maya cooks use every morning to crush lime-soaked maize into dough? [ A stone metate / An iron windmill ]
Q14. How did royal Maya hosts flavour their frothy cacao drink, and how else were dried cacao beans used?
Q15. Which shiny black volcanic glass did highland Maya traders swap for razor-sharp cutting blades?
Q16. How did Maya traders transport heavy jade, salt, and feathers without horses, oxen, or wheeled carts?
Enquiry 5: Maize Gods, Rain Caves & Chichén Itzá — How Did Maya Beliefs Shape Their World? (c. AD 600–1200)
Q17. According to the sacred Maya book Popol Vuh, what ingredient did the gods successfully use to make human beings? [ Yellow and white maize dough / Solid blocks of river ice ]
Q18. What did the Maya call the eerie watery underworld where the Hero Twins outsmarted its fearsome lords?
Q19. Which long-nosed Maya god controlled life-giving rain, thunder, and lightning?
Q20. Why do El Castillo’s four staircases (4 × 91 steps) plus its top temple platform add up to 365?
Enquiry 6: The AD 900 Mystery & Living Maya Today — Did the Maya Really Vanish? (c. AD 800–Present)
Q21. What scientific clue from lake-bed mud and snail shells helped explain why southern cities like Tikal emptied around AD 900?
Q22. Besides drought and warfare, how did burning huge amounts of white lime plaster for pyramids hurt Maya cities?
Q23. Which walled Postclassic Maya city perched on Caribbean Sea cliffs thrived with canoe trade centuries after AD 900?
Q24. What do historians call an explanation that links several causes together — like drought, deforestation, and war?
