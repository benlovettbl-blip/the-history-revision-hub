Central Enquiry: Did Roman water engineering protect ordinary Britons or showcase imperial luxury?
Curriculum / Spec Anchor: KS3 Thematic Study (Pre-1066 Baseline): Roman Britain (AD 43–410) — evaluating change, continuity, and the limits of imperial public health engineering.
