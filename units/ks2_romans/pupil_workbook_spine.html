The Romans in Britain

“How did the Roman Empire conquer Britain and change life on our island forever?”
3 Clues to Spot in This Mosaic: • Winged Cupid riding a leaping dolphin while holding a trident.
• Mythical sea-horses, sea-panthers, and two-handled Roman wine jars.
• Thousands of tiny coloured stone cubes (tesserae) and rope borders.
Think About This: Did life change equally for wealthy villa owners walking on mosaic floors and rural British farmers living in roundhouses?
