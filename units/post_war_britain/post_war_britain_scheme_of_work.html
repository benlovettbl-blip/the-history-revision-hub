Core Learning Objectives
  • Explain how the experience of total war and evacuation led directly to the publication of the 1942 Beveridge Report.
  • Evaluate why Clement Attlee's Labour Party defeated Winston Churchill in the July 1945 general election.
  • Analyse how Aneurin Bevan overcame the British Medical Association to establish the NHS on 5 July 1948.
