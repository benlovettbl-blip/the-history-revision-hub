John White (Watercolour) (1585) The British Museum, London (1906,0509.1.21)
Cover Source Study 1585
‘A cheife Herowan’ — Algonquian Leader at Roanoke (1585)
Painted from life on Roanoke Island during Raleigh’s 1585 Virginia expedition, John White’s watercolour records Secotan leadership before English food demands sparked fatal violence.
3 Details to Spot in This Source:
1. Square copper gorget on a neck cord — prized trade metal English settlers bartered for corn.
2. Shaved roach hairstyle, upright feather, and body markings denoting elite Secotan rank.
3. Composed folded-arm stance painted from life before Ralph Lane’s ambush killed Wingina.
Class Discussion: Why did English dependence on Algonquian food supplies rapidly turn initial curiosity—captured in John White’s 1585 watercolours—into fatal conflict at Roanoke?
Q1 • Two Features 4m (2×2m)
Two separate 2-mark features (1a & 1b). Identify a valid feature (1m) + add 1 sentence of specific factual detail (1m) (5 mins).
Q2 • Explain Why 12 Marks
3 PEEL causation paragraphs explaining why. Include own knowledge beyond the 2 stimulus points — zero storytelling (18 mins).
Q3 / Q4 • How Far Agree 16 Marks
Choose Q3 OR Q4 (zero SPaG). Write 3 balanced paragraphs (for & against + own knowledge) and a clear judgement (27 mins).
